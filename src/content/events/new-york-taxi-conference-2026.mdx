The New York Taxi Conference is the largest annual gathering of the people who keep the city's yellow and green cabs moving. This year the focus is on the humble taxi rank: where they go, how they work, and why they still matter in an app-dominated market.

## What to expect

- **Keynotes** from TLC commissioners, DOT curb planners, and veteran drivers
- **Panels** on rank placement, congestion pricing, and EV charging at the curb
- **Workshops** for fleet operators on dispatch, data, and driver retention
- **An expo floor** with medallion lenders, insurers, and vehicle manufacturers

## Who should attend

Drivers, medallion owners, fleet managers, city officials, transit advocates, and anyone who has ever waited in a taxi line at Penn Station and thought "there has to be a better way."

## Getting there

The Javits Center is a short walk from the 34 St-Hudson Yards station on the 7 line. A temporary taxi rank will operate on 11th Avenue throughout both days.
