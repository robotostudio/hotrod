JFK moves more taxi passengers than any other site in the city. With new terminals coming online, the holding lot, dispatcher booths, and terminal ranks are all changing. This summit brings together Port Authority staff, ground transportation dispatchers, and drivers to talk through what is coming.

## Agenda

1. **Holding lot 101:** how the central taxi hold works today and where the bottlenecks are
2. **Terminal by terminal:** rank layouts for Terminals 1, 4, 5, 7, and 8
3. **Short-trip return program:** proposed changes and driver feedback
4. **Open floor Q&A** with Port Authority ground transportation

Breakfast is served from 8:00 AM. Driver attendees can park in the taxi hold and take the AirTrain to the hotel.
