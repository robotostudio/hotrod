The New York Taxi Conference brings the whole taxi world into one room. Drivers, fleet owners, dispatchers and city planners sit side by side to look at how ranks shape every trip in the city.

## What to expect

- Talks on how ranks are placed, signed and managed across the five boroughs
- Panels with drivers who work the busiest ranks every day
- Open sessions where you can raise the rank problems you see most

## Who it's for

Anyone who drives, runs or plans around New York taxis, and anyone curious about how a yellow cab finds its next fare.
