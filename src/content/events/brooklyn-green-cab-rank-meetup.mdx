Green cabs were created to serve the parts of the city yellow cabs rarely reach. But most of Brooklyn still has no formal taxi ranks at all. This community meetup is a chance to change that.

## How it works

We will split into small groups by neighborhood, each with a large printed map. Drivers and residents will mark the corners where riders actually wait for cabs, where pickups are dangerous, and where a marked rank would make life easier for everyone.

## What happens next

Every pin gets collected and shared with Brooklyn community boards and the DOT. Last year's Queens meetup led to four new pilot ranks in Jackson Heights and Flushing.

Bring your local knowledge. We will bring the pizza.
