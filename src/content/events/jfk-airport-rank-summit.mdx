Airport ranks are a world of their own. Long queues, holding lots and waves of arriving passengers all have to meet at the curb.

## On the agenda

- How airport queues move, and where they stall
- Keeping the rank fair for drivers who have waited the longest
- Making the walk from arrivals to the cab clear for first-time visitors

Come ready to share what works at the airport rank, and what doesn't.
