Every ferry arrival at St. George sends a wave of passengers toward the taxi rank, and then the curb goes quiet for twenty minutes. The DOT has drafted a redesign to smooth out those peaks, and this open house is your chance to weigh in.

## At the open house

- **Full-size boards** showing the current rank and the proposed new layout
- **Ferry-synced dispatch:** a demo of a screen that tells drivers when the next boat lands
- **Planner office hours:** drop in anytime between 11 AM and 3 PM
- **Feedback stations** where you can leave written comments or sketches

## Why it matters

St. George is the main gateway to Staten Island for thousands of commuters and visitors every day. A rank that works with the ferry schedule, instead of against it, means shorter waits for riders and less idling for drivers.
