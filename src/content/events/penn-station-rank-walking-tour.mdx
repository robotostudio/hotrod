Penn Station is one of the busiest taxi pickup points in North America, and also one of the most confusing. On this two-hour walk we visit every official rank around the station and talk about why each one exists.

## Stops on the tour

- **Moynihan Train Hall, 8th Avenue:** the newest rank and how it was designed
- **7th Avenue at 32nd Street:** the classic Penn line, and why it backs up
- **Madison Square Garden event rank:** how surge crowds are handled on game nights
- **31st Street dispatcher booth:** a peek behind the curtain at manual dispatch

## Your guides

Our guides are a yellow cab driver who has worked the Penn line since 1996 and a DOT planner responsible for Midtown curb space. Expect strong opinions from both.
