A rank only works if everyone can use it. This workshop looks at the rank from the rider's side of the curb.

## In the workshop

- Walking a rank together and spotting what gets in the way
- Clear signs, level curbs and enough space to board
- How drivers and riders can help each other at pickup

You'll leave with a simple checklist you can use at any rank in the city.
