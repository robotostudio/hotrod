The city never sleeps, and neither do its ranks. This meetup is for the drivers who keep New York moving between dusk and dawn.

## What we'll cover

- Staying safe at quiet ranks late at night
- Which ranks pick up when the theatres and venues let out
- Looking after yourself on long night shifts

Grab a coffee, meet other night drivers and trade what you've learned on the road.
