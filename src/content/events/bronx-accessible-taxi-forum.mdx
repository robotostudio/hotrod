A taxi rank is only useful if everyone can use it. This forum focuses on the experience of riders who use wheelchairs, walkers, or other mobility aids, and on what drivers need to serve them well.

## Topics

- **Rank design:** curb cuts, ramp space, and signage that works
- **Accessible Dispatch:** how the citywide program is performing in the Bronx
- **Driver training and incentives** for wheelchair-accessible vehicles
- **Rider testimony:** an open mic for Bronx residents to share their experiences

## Venue access

Bronx Borough Hall is fully step-free from the Grand Concourse entrance. The 161 St-Yankee Stadium station has elevators on the 4, B, and D lines. A marked accessible taxi rank is located directly outside.
