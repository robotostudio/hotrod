Brooklyn's streets ask a lot of the curb. Delivery vans, bike lanes, buses and taxis all want the same few feet of space.

## The conversation

- Where taxi ranks fit on busy shopping streets
- Safe pickups and drop-offs near stations and schools
- How local businesses can work with drivers, not against them

Bring your questions and your street. Every block has a story.
